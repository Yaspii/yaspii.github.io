Campaign 01 · In the heat

Made to move at 32°C.

Early runs through Bangkok, long rides by the sea. The first pictures from our campaign, with more sports on the way.

    What we are fixing

    Three things your leggings get wrong after one humid month.

    Odor

    They keep the smell

    Synthetic fabrics hold odor after a few sweaty sessions. We are testing fabrics and finishes that wash clean every time.

    Sheerness

    They go see-through

    Thin, stretched fabric turns sheer in a squat. Every Yaspii bottom gets a squat test under daylight before it ships.

    Fit

    The sizes don't match you

    A medium means something different at every brand. We build on an Asian fit block and publish real garment measurements, in cm.
